## Goals:

1. **Create fun game**:  Have a finished product which i'm confident in publishing to the public.

2. **Finish on time**:  Complete the game within the set time limit for the jam.

## Reflection:

Over my winter break, I decided to participate in a game jam!  With no school for about a month, I had plenty of time to kill.  What better way to spend it then a quick challenge?  I decided to team up with my younger brother to help motivate myself to continue working over the course of the week and to get him to hang out with me.  It ended up fixing both problems.

### The Jam

We ended up choosing the [Beginners Jam Winter 2025](https://itch.io/jam/beginners-jam-winter-2025) to participate in, as my brother has little experience with actually developing games.  The theme was "Silent Night," which reminded us of an old Wii Fit game where you would sit in front of a candle and try to stay as still as possible to keep it from going out.  It had an oddly scary atmosphere for a Wii game, which we felt set it apart in our memory.

Inspired by Lotus Focus, we set to work on our own version, collecting CC models from online to fill out our game world.  We also decided together to work in the Godot engine, due to its free and open source nature (Big plusses for us).  Our version of the spooky nighttime candle scene turned out pretty good (Keep in mind me and my brother both aren't impressive artists)!

![Fragile Focus](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvNDE2NDU0MS8yNDgyMjQzMC5wbmc=/original/nGiw3I.png)

While the game looked cool, its wasn't very interactive.  We decided to make the game about defending your candle from various threats such as rain, flies, flying balloons, and rumbling trains (We had some pretty weird ideas).  We kept adding and tweaking all of these various threats until we were happy with the balance of the game and the fun-factor.  It turned late-game into a very frantic rush to try and keep your candle safe from everything!

### Final Product

In the end, we were left with a very functional and fun game!  It's available to play on itch.io [here](https://slmply.itch.io/silent-night).  Leave a comment about what you think, and possibly what your longest time!
